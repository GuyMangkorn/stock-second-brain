## Transcript / Management Commentary

Official commentary frames CEG as a larger post-Calpine platform with assets that management says are difficult to replicate. The Q1 presentation emphasizes 20%+ Base EPS growth through 2029, long-term rolling three-year Base EPS growth of 10%+, growth capital, and expanded buyback authorization.

The positive signal is that demand for reliable clean / firm power appears strong and CEG has multiple ways to monetize it: capacity markets, large-load bilateral contracting, powered land, data centers, gas capacity, battery/storage, and nuclear uprates. The caveat is that the disclosed FCF bridge is split: GAAP-style TTM `OCF - capex` is only USD 1.137B, while management highlights non-GAAP `free cash flow before growth` of USD 8.4B across 2026-2027.

## Thesis

### Bull Case

CEG is one of the most direct listed beneficiaries of U.S. power demand growth. The combination of nuclear scarcity, Calpine dispatchable gas assets, data-center load growth, PJM / ERCOT policy clarity, and large-load contracting could produce a multi-year earnings and cash-flow step-up. If management delivers 20%+ Base EPS growth through 2029 and FCF before growth scales from USD 8.4B in 2026-2027 to USD 11.5B-13.0B in 2028-2029, today's premium may be partly justified.

### Bear Case

Valuation and cash-flow definition are the core problems. At USD 287.75, market cap is about USD 104.27B and market EV about USD 125.56B. That is roughly 29.9x management's average 2026-2027 FCF before growth and more than 100x GAAP-style TTM FCF. Leverage also stepped up materially after Calpine, with total debt used locally at USD 22.466B and net debt at USD 21.295B.

Regulatory and market design risk matter. PJM / ERCOT large-load rules, nuclear PTC mechanics, co-location treatment, capacity prices, gas spreads, and data-center contracting economics can all move value materially. A good theme can still be a poor entry if the stock already prices a near-perfect path.

### Key Debate

คำถามหลักคือ CEG ควรถูก underwrite ด้วย `free cash flow before growth` แบบ owner earnings หรือควร haircut หนักเพราะ growth capex, integration, collateral, merchant power cyclicality, and regulatory uncertainty. ถ้า FCF before growth กลายเป็น recurring cash available หลังลงทุนที่จำเป็นแล้ว stock อาจยังมี upside; ถ้าไม่ใช่ valuation จะดูแพงมาก.

## Risks

- Valuation risk: market EV / FCF before growth around 29.9x and market EV / TTM GAAP-style FCF above 100x.
- Leverage risk: net debt rose to about USD 21.295B after Calpine.
- Integration risk from Calpine and fleet divestitures / capital structure changes.
- Regulatory risk across PJM, ERCOT, nuclear PTC, co-location, large-load procurement, and state / federal energy policy.
- Commodity and power-price risk from capacity prices, spark spreads, gas prices, weather, hedging, collateral, and load-serving costs.
- Cash-flow quality risk because `free cash flow before growth` is non-GAAP and not the same as `OCF - capex`.
- Operational risk from nuclear outages, forced outage rates, gas plant availability, and project execution.

## Catalysts

- Q2 2026 results showing whether Calpine integration, debt, OCF, capex, and FCF conversion improve.
- PJM reliability backstop procurement timeline and regulatory clarity.
- Additional large-load / data-center contracting announcements with clear economics.
- Evidence that FCF before growth converts into cash after required growth capex and collateral needs.
- Share repurchase execution under the USD 5.0B authorization.
- Deleveraging or clearer investment-grade balance sheet progress.
- Price pullback that lifts FCF-before-growth yield and improves margin of safety.
