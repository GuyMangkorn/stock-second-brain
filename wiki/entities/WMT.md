## Business Model

Walmart เป็น global omnichannel retailer ขนาดใหญ่มากที่ใช้ store base, clubs, fulfillment network, marketplace, membership, advertising, data services, และ last-mile delivery เพื่อขายสินค้า essential และ general merchandise ในหลายประเทศ. Core economics ยังเป็น low-margin, high-turnover retail model แต่ management กำลังพยายามยกระดับ mix ด้วย eCommerce economics, advertising, membership fees, marketplace, VIZIO, data services, automation, และ AI-assisted commerce.

FY2026 total revenues อยู่ที่ USD 713.163B และ consolidated operating margin บน total revenues อยู่ราว 4.2%. ดังนั้น investment case ไม่ใช่ margin สูงตั้งแต่ต้น แต่คือ scale, traffic, purchasing power, working-capital discipline, และ optionality จาก higher-margin businesses ที่ค่อยๆ ทำให้ profit growth โตเร็วกว่า sales ได้.

## Transcript / Management Commentary

Management framed FY2026 as a year where revenue exceeded USD 700B and profit growth continued to benefit from business mix. The Q4 call emphasized eCommerce growth, advertising, membership income, marketplace, VIZIO, data services, automation, delivery speed, and AI-assisted shopping as the drivers that could raise profit contribution over time.

The best operating signal is that management is trying to make the digital business less margin-dilutive by attaching higher-margin monetization layers. The caution is that the disclosed facts still show a very low FCF yield at the current stock price, and product-level profitability for these higher-margin engines is not disclosed.

## Thesis

### Bull Case

WMT คือ defensive scale compounder ที่มี traffic, grocery frequency, purchasing power, and omnichannel reach สูงมาก. FY2027 guidance calls for constant-currency net sales growth of 3.5%-4.5% and adjusted operating income growth of 6.0%-8.0%, implying operating leverage from business mix. eCommerce, advertising, membership, marketplace, and automation can let profit grow faster than sales if management keeps execution tight.

### Bear Case

ราคาหุ้นสะท้อน quality มากเกินไปแล้ว. At USD 132.57, implied market cap is about USD 1.057T and FY2026 FCF yield is only about 1.4%. Base DCF using source-backed FY2026 FCF gives fair value around USD 37 per diluted share, far below market. Even if WMT deserves premium multiples for defensive quality, the current price leaves little room for disappointment before FY2027 Q1 actuals.

### Key Debate

คำถามหลักคือ higher-margin growth engines จะเพิ่ม FCF per share ได้เร็วพอที่จะ justify valuation ระดับ mega-cap premium หรือไม่. ถ้า advertising, membership, marketplace, and automation scale into cash flow, current valuation can look less extreme. ถ้า capex stays heavy and FCF growth lags adjusted EPS, downside risk remains material.

## Risks

- Valuation risk: forward adjusted P/E around 47x midpoint FY2027 adjusted EPS guidance and FY2026 FCF yield around 1.4%.
- Execution risk: eCommerce, ads, marketplace, membership, VIZIO, and data services must scale without eroding customer value proposition.
- Margin risk from tariffs, wage/labor cost, claims expense, product mix, and price investment.
- Capex intensity risk: FY2027 capex guidance is approximately 3.5% of net sales.
- Competitive risk from Amazon, Costco, Target, dollar stores, regional grocers, and international competitors.
- Currency and emerging-market risk in Walmart International.
- Regulatory, labor, data privacy, antitrust, and supply-chain risks typical of a global retailer.

## Catalysts
