## Business Model

McDonald's franchises and operates restaurants in more than 100 countries. The model is heavily franchised: approximately 95% of restaurants were franchised at 2026-03-31. The company earns revenue from franchised restaurant rents, royalties, and initial fees, plus sales from Company-owned restaurants and other revenues from technology/digital platforms and brand licensing.

| Model element | Economics | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional franchise | McDonald's generally owns or secures long-term lease on land/building; franchisee invests in equipment/signage/seating/decor and pays rent/royalty. | Q1 2026 Form 10-Q. |
| Developmental license / affiliate | Licensees generally provide capital and operate/develop restaurants; McDonald's receives royalties and initial fees. | Q1 2026 Form 10-Q. |
| Company-operated restaurants | Gives operational credibility and a testing ground for operating standards, marketing, product, and pricing strategies. | Q1 2026 Form 10-Q. |
| Strategy | `Accelerating the Arches`: Marketing, Core menu, Digital/Delivery/Drive Thru/Development. | FY2025 Form 10-K / Q1 2026 Form 10-Q. |

## Thesis

### Bull Case

MCD มี brand และ unit economics ที่หายาก: global scale, heavily franchised revenue streams, rent/royalty economics, real estate control, drive-thru footprint, digital loyalty, and capital allocation discipline. Q1 2026 showed real operational resilience with global comparable sales +3.8%, revenue +9.4%, and operating income +11.5%. Management still guides 2026 operating margin in the mid-to-high 40% range and expects low-to-mid 80% FCF conversion.

ถ้า restaurant development, loyalty, delivery, and menu innovation keep compounding while margins stay high, MCD deserves a premium versus ordinary restaurant operators.

### Bear Case

Valuation คือปัญหาใหญ่. At USD 282.27, MCD trades at only 3.51% TTM FCF yield and about 36.1x market EV / TTM FCF. Core net debt is high at about 5.5x TTM FCF, and lease-adjusted obligations push that context higher. Q1 FCF fell YoY because capex increased, and management is guiding 2026 capex to USD 3.7B-3.9B as restaurant development accelerates.

สำหรับ new capital, quality is obvious but margin of safety is not.

### Key Debate

คำถามหลักคือ premium multiple ควรสูงแค่ไหนสำหรับ franchise-quality business ที่ยังโตได้ แต่มี debt/lease obligations และ FCF yield ต่ำ. ถ้า investor ใช้ lower WACC มากเพราะ business durability สูง หุ้นอาจดู holdable. ถ้าใช้ required return แบบ conservative, base-case DCF ต่ำกว่าราคาตลาดมาก.

## Risks

- Premium valuation leaves little room for disappointment.
- Consumer pressure, especially lower-income households, can affect traffic, value perception, and guest counts.
- Franchisee economics are central but not fully disclosed.
- High debt and lease obligations raise sensitivity to interest rates and funding costs.
- Accelerated restaurant development increases capex and execution risk.
- Commodity, labor, supply chain, food safety, and geopolitical risks can affect Systemwide sales and margins.

## Catalysts

- Q2/Q3 2026 results confirming comparable-sales growth across all segments.
- Evidence that unit growth and digital/loyalty initiatives improve guest counts, not just average check.
- FCF conversion meeting or exceeding low-to-mid 80% guidance despite higher capex.
- Stronger franchisee health disclosures or sustained rent/royalty growth.
- Share price pullback that improves FCF yield and DCF margin of safety.
- Progress toward 50,000 global restaurants by end-2027 without margin dilution.
