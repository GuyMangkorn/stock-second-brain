## ETF Notes

### VIG - Vanguard Dividend Appreciation ETF

- Theme: U.S. dividend growth / quality; tracks companies with long dividend growth history and excludes extreme high yielders.
- Asset country: mostly U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: mega-cap quality compounders, technology platforms, health care, financials, energy, consumer staples.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Broadcom, Microsoft, Apple, Eli Lilly, JPMorgan Chase, Visa, Exxon Mobil.
- Dividend past / yield: trailing yield around `1.7%` in recent 2026 secondary-source screen; Vanguard ETFs generally distribute dividends quarterly for equity ETFs.
- Score: `8.0/10`. เหมาะกว่า high-yield ETF ใน Fed hawkish เพราะหลบ yield trap และมี quality/growth buffer แต่ไม่ใช่ income-heavy fund.

### VYM - Vanguard High Dividend Yield ETF

- Theme: U.S. broad high dividend yield, excluding REIT-style yield traps in the index methodology cited by secondary source.
- Asset country: mostly U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: financials, large-cap tech dividend payers, health care, consumer staples, energy.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Broadcom, JPMorgan Chase, Exxon Mobil, Procter & Gamble, Johnson & Johnson.
- Dividend past / yield: recent 2026 high-yield ETF screen shows yield around `2.4%`.
- Score: `7.0/10`. ใช้เป็น income sleeve ได้ แต่ถ้า Fed hawkish จริง กลุ่ม financial/value/cyclical อาจผันผวนกว่ากอง dividend growth.

### DGRO - iShares Core Dividend Growth ETF

- Theme: U.S. dividend growth with sustainability screens; benchmark is Morningstar U.S. Dividend Growth Index.
- Asset country: U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: financials, health care, information technology, industrials, consumer staples.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Microsoft, Apple, JPMorgan Chase, Exxon Mobil, Johnson & Johnson / Procter & Gamble-type large dividend growers.
- Dividend past / yield: official iShares page showed `30 Day SEC Yield 1.97%` and `12m Trailing Yield 1.96%` as of 2026-05-31; distribution frequency is quarterly.
- Score: `8.0/10`. ดีสุดร่วมกับ VIG สำหรับ long-term hold เพราะ balance ระหว่าง dividend growth, quality, cost, และ lower yield-trap risk.

### DVY - iShares Select Dividend ETF

- Theme: U.S. high dividend / dividend-weighted equity income.
- Asset country: U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: utilities, financials, consumer staples, energy, communication / telecom-like income stocks.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Altria, Verizon, AT&T, regional utilities, energy / financial income stocks.
- Dividend past / yield: ไม่พบข้อมูลที่ยืนยันได้ใน quick official-page pass; historically positioned as higher-yield U.S. equity income.
- Score: `5.5/10`. Yield น่าสนใจแต่ utility / high-yield factor มี duration-like behavior และเสี่ยง underperform เมื่อ rates ขึ้น.

### SDY - SPDR S&P Dividend ETF

- Theme: U.S. Dividend Aristocrats-style exposure from S&P Composite 1500 companies with long dividend increase records; yield-weighted.
- Asset country: U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: industrials, consumer staples, utilities, financials, health care.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Microchip Technology, Verizon, Realty Income, utilities, industrial dividend growers.
- Dividend past / yield: recent 2026 dividend-growth screen shows yield around `2.5%`.
- Score: `6.0/10`. Durable dividend history helps, but yield weighting and utilities/REIT-like exposure make it less attractive than VIG/DGRO in hawkish Fed.

### VYMI - Vanguard International High Dividend Yield ETF

- Theme: non-U.S. high dividend yield.
- Asset country: international developed markets plus some emerging markets; major country exposure historically includes Europe / Pacific / Canada / Japan-style developed dividend markets.
- Top-10 theme read: international financials, energy, materials, telecom, health care, consumer staples.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Nestle, Roche / Novartis-type Swiss health care and consumer staples, Toyota / Japanese cyclicals, European banks, Canadian banks.
- Dividend past / yield: secondary 2026 screen cited `VYMI` yield around `4.2%`.
- Score: `6.5/10`. Good diversification and income, but FX, country tax treatment, banks/energy cyclicality, and global rates keep it satellite-only.

- Theme: Taiwan high dividend / selected Taiwan equity income.
- Asset country: Taiwan equities.
- Top-10 theme read: Taiwan semiconductor supply chain, electronics manufacturing, financials, telecom, industrial cyclicals.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Taiwan high-dividend semiconductor/electronics names and large financials; exact holding list needs current Capital / TWSE source confirmation before order.
- Dividend past / yield: ไม่พบข้อมูลที่ยืนยันได้ใน quick pass; Taiwanese high-dividend ETFs often advertise frequent distributions, but sustainability must be checked against realized income and capital return policy.

- Theme: U.S. energy infrastructure MLPs / midstream.
- Asset country: U.S. listed MLP / energy infrastructure securities.
- Top-10 theme read: pipelines, natural gas liquids, midstream transport/storage, energy infrastructure partnerships.
- Example top holdings / names to verify before trade: Enterprise Products Partners, Energy Transfer, MPLX, Plains All American, Western Midstream-type MLPs.
- Dividend past / yield: official ALPS page states quarterly distribution frequency and reports trailing-twelve-month distribution / SEC yield fields; exact current value was not visible in quick text scrape. The page notes a portion of TTM distribution as of 2026-03-31 was return of capital.
- Score: `5.5/10`. Cash yield can be attractive, but hawkish Fed raises cost-of-capital pressure; sector, tax structure, commodity volume, and regulation risks make it tactical.

### NOBL - ProShares S&P 500 Dividend Aristocrats ETF

- Theme: S&P 500 Dividend Aristocrats; companies with at least 25 years of dividend increases.
- Asset country: U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: health care, consumer staples, industrials, financials, materials; broad quality dividend stalwarts.
- Example top holdings from official ProShares holdings as of 2026-06-26: West Pharmaceutical Services, Hormel Foods, Caterpillar, AbbVie, J.M. Smucker, Franklin Resources, Stanley Black & Decker, Essex Property Trust, Cardinal Health, W.W. Grainger.
- Dividend past / yield: official ProShares page showed quarterly distribution frequency, `SEC 30-Day Yield 2.28%`, and `12-Month Yield 2.12%` as of 2026-05-31.
- Score: `6.5/10`. Good discipline and dividend durability, but 0.35% fee and equal/dividend-aristocrat constraints may lag lower-cost VIG/DGRO.

- Theme: U.S. high dividend with quality / consistency / growth-potential screen.
- Asset country: U.S. equities.
- Top-10 theme read: mega-cap quality dividend payers, financials, technology, consumer staples, energy.
