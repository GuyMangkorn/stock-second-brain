# Decision Memos

Store add, hold, wait, trim, avoid, and watchlist decision memos here.

Use:

```text
wiki/analysis/decisions/TICKER Decision Memo YYYY-MM-DD.md
wiki/analysis/decisions/ETF_EXCHANGE_TICKER Decision Memo YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

ETF decisions use `Portfolio Role`, `BUY/WATCH/AVOID`, current price/NAV,
peer-relative evidence, and a valuation/cost/tracking read. They do not use
corporate DCF or claim portfolio fit without user-provided portfolio holdings.
