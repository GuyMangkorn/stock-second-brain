## Decision-Changing Evidence

Q1 2026 revenue grew 56% to USD 19.799bn, with volume +65% but price effect -13%. Mounjaro revenue grew 125% to USD 8.662bn and Zepbound grew 80% to USD 4.160bn; together they were 64.8% of Q1 revenue on the normalized product table. The business is delivering real demand, but the price/volume mix shows why growth cannot be read from volume alone.

Cash conversion improved: Q1 2026 FCF was USD 3.007bn versus USD 0.156bn in Q1 2025, and FY2025 FCF was USD 8.972bn. The counterweight is reinvestment and capital structure: FY2025 capex was USD 7.841bn, Q1 2026 capex was USD 2.326bn, total debt was USD 43.370bn at March 31, and pending acquisitions could require up to approximately USD 12bn at closing. Product-level profitability and cash returns on the manufacturing buildout are not disclosed.

The positive variant is not only tirzepatide. Foundayo is an approved oral obesity medicine, the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge began July 1, and June official data showed strong Foundayo type 2 diabetes results and positive retatrutide Phase 3 outcomes. Jaypirca received a CHMP positive opinion for CLL, and AAIC 2026 will provide Kisunla/diagnostic data. These are meaningful catalysts, but much of the value is forward-looking and already embedded in the price.

The near-term counterweight is an official preliminary estimate of approximately USD 2.8bn of Q2 acquired IPR&D charges, or approximately USD 3.02/share, before final closing. It is not a recurring FCF item, but it can make Q2 reported and non-GAAP EPS look weaker. The Q2 earnings call is scheduled for August 5, 2026.

## Valuation Read

P11 passed with a high-sensitivity FCF DCF. The model uses FY2025 FCF as the anchor, explicit five-year growth, 9.0% base WACC, 3.0% terminal growth, USD 5.282bn cash, USD 43.370bn debt, and approximately 895m diluted shares from guidance. Base fair value is USD 248.73/share; bear is USD 111.21; bull is USD 385.30. Terminal value is 78.0% of base EV, so this is a scenario read rather than a precise target.

The multiple cross-check is also demanding: 32.8x FY2026 non-GAAP EPS midpoint and about 129x EV/FY2025 FCF. The bull DCF remains far below the market price, so the stock price appears to require a much stronger long-run FCF path than the current source-backed base can justify.

## Key Assumption / Falsifier

The key assumption for a future upgrade is that Foundayo access and uptake, continued tirzepatide volume, and pipeline launches produce FCF growth faster than price pressure and manufacturing investment consume it. The view is falsified if Q2 shows material volume deceleration, lower realized prices without offsetting access/volume, weaker FCF after capex, manufacturing delays, or safety/regulatory setbacks in Foundayo, retatrutide, Jaypirca, or Kisunla. Even a positive Q2 does not by itself justify ADD if the price remains far above the valuation range.
