## Decision-Changing Evidence

1Q26 net income was $16.5B, diluted EPS $5.94, ROE 19%, and ROTCE 23%. CIB generated $9.0B net income on $23.4B revenue, while AWM generated $1.8B and AUM reached $4.8T with net inflows. Loans and deposits grew YoY, supporting the breadth of the franchise.

The counterweight is that Markets and investment-banking strength may be cyclical. Management expects FY2026 total NII of about $103B, NII excluding Markets of about $95B, adjusted expense of about $105B, and Card Services NCO of about 3.4%. Standardized CET1 was 14.3% versus 15.4% a year earlier, while 1Q26 RWA increased. Lower rates, credit-cycle losses, geopolitical stress, and capital-rule changes remain material risks.

Capital return is supportive but not a standalone buy signal: the Board intended to raise the quarterly dividend to $1.65 for 3Q26, subject to approval, and authorized a $50B repurchase program effective 2026-07-01.

P11 ผ่าน stage gate using a bank-specific P/TBV model. Base fair value is $304.84 at 2.8x TBVPS; bear is $250.40 at 2.3x; bull is $359.27 at 3.3x. ## Key Assumption / Falsifier

The key assumption is that durable 20%+ ROTCE justifies a premium multiple near or above the base case. The view is falsified if 2Q26 shows weaker NII/expense outlook, Card NCO deterioration, a materially narrower CET1 buffer, or lower ROTCE without an offsetting improvement in TBVPS growth or capital returns.
