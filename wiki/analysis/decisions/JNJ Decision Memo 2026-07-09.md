# JNJ Decision Memo - 2026-07-09

## Action Read

**Action: WAIT / DO NOT ADD before Q2 2026 results.**

JNJ ยังเป็น quality defensive health care compounder แต่ stock rerating เร็วกว่าหลักฐาน cash-flow ใหม่ใน vault. ราคาล่าสุดที่ตรวจได้คือ USD 263.40 ณ close 2026-07-08 เทียบกับ USD 228.92 ใน decision memo เดิม เพิ่มขึ้นประมาณ 15.1% ขณะที่ official-source financial anchor ยังเป็น Q1 2026 และ FY2026 FCF outlook ประมาณ USD 21B.

## Bull Case

- Q1 2026 sales grew 9.9% reported and 6.4% operational despite STELARA pressure.
- Management raised FY2026 sales and adjusted EPS guidance in April.
- FY2026 FCF outlook of approximately USD 21B supports dividend durability.
- Innovative Medicine has multiple growth drivers: DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, ERLEADA, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE, TREMFYA, SPRAVATO, ICOTYDE, CAPLYTA, and INLEXZO.
- MedTech growth drivers include electrophysiology, Abiomed, Shockwave, cardiovascular, trauma, and vision.
- Defensive quality and dividend-growth history can attract capital in volatile markets.

## Bear Case

- At USD 263.40, valuation is demanding: about 30.2x FY2026 FCF outlook and 22.8x FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance midpoint.
- Q1 FCF was only USD 1.465B, so the full-year USD 21B FCF outlook still needs back-half conversion evidence.
- STELARA remains an explicit headwind; Q1 management commentary said it was a meaningful drag.
- MedTech still has tariff, separation-cost, China VBP, and integration execution risks.
- Net debt was about USD 32.936B at Q1 2026, so capital allocation flexibility is not unlimited.
- Talc/legal matters can distort GAAP earnings and investor perception.

## What Would Change The Decision

- Upgrade toward add only if price falls materially or Q2 raises FY2026 FCF outlook enough to improve FCF yield and DCF support.
- Upgrade if Q2 shows clean FCF conversion, net debt reduction, and stronger launch momentum that offsets STELARA without margin deterioration.
- Upgrade if MedTech tariff/separation pressure fades and cardiovascular growth remains strong.
- Downgrade toward trim if Q2 FCF disappoints, guidance is not raised, debt rises, legal risk worsens, or the stock rerates further without new cash-flow evidence.
