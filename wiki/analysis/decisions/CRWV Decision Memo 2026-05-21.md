## Valuation Read

No precise DCF fair value was calculated. The verified FCF base is negative and no official FY2026 FCF guidance was found. A conventional DCF here would mainly be a bet on the analyst's capex-normalization assumption, not a source-backed valuation.

The best source-backed read today is multiple/cash-flow risk: EV / FY2026 revenue guidance midpoint is about 7.05x, while TTM FCF is negative USD 10.616B. That can still work for a high-growth infrastructure platform, but the margin of safety is not visible from durable source facts yet.

## Bull Case

- Revenue backlog of USD 99.355B and RPO of USD 98.767B give unusually strong top-line visibility.
- FY2026 revenue guidance of USD 12B to USD 13B implies a major acceleration from FY2025 revenue of USD 5.131B.
- AI compute demand remains capacity constrained, and CoreWeave is positioned as a specialized provider.
- Adjusted EBITDA is already large, with Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA of USD 1.157B.
- If capex converts into durable high-utilization contracted capacity, current FCF could be temporarily depressed rather than structurally negative.

## Bear Case

- FCF is deeply negative: FY2025 FCF USD -7.251B, Q1 2026 FCF USD -4.711B, and TTM FCF USD -10.616B.
- FY2026 capex guidance of USD 31B to USD 35B is very large relative to revenue guidance.
- Debt-like obligations including leases were USD 35.147B at 2026-03-31, before fully verified post-quarter pro forma debt.
- Interest expense is material, with Q2 2026 guidance of USD 650M to USD 730M.
- Customer-level economics and segment-level FCF are not disclosed.
- Public trading history is short, so valuation anchors are thin and sentiment-driven.

## Key Assumptions

- Demand remains strong enough to absorb the planned capacity build.
- Financing remains available and does not overly dilute equity or consume economics through interest expense.
- Backlog converts into revenue, cash collection, and eventually positive FCF.
- FY2026 capex guidance is not materially exceeded.
- Maintenance capex after the buildout is materially lower than current growth capex, though this is not yet source-backed.

## What Would Change The Decision

- Upgrade toward WATCHLIST-for-entry if price falls sharply while official sources show no deterioration in backlog, revenue guidance, or liquidity.
- Upgrade toward ADD only after positive normalized FCF or a credible official FCF bridge becomes source-backed.
- Re-run P11 if management discloses maintenance capex, long-term FCF targets, or clearer returns on deployed capacity.
- Downgrade further if debt, leases, interest expense, or capex rise faster than revenue / adjusted EBITDA guidance.
- Downgrade if backlog conversion slows, customer risk becomes concentrated, or equity dilution accelerates.
