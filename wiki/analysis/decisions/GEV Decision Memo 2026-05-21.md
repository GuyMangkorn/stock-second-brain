# GEV Decision Memo - 2026-05-21

**Action: WAIT / AVOID-new-capital. HOLD only if already owned and thesis-aware.**

## Bull Case

- Power demand, AI/data-center load growth, electrification, and grid modernization are durable multi-year drivers.
- Backlog / RPO around USD 163B gives revenue visibility.
- Power and Electrification are already profitable and can scale margin if supply/delivery execution holds.
- Wind losses may narrow, creating operating leverage from a depressed base.
- Balance sheet is net cash, reducing financial-risk pressure.
- Customer down payments can be a funding advantage if orders convert cleanly.

## Bear Case

- Current valuation is already rich: forward guided FCF yield is about 2.54%.
- Wind remains loss-making and can keep absorbing management attention and cash.
- Q1 FCF quality is partly working-capital / down-payment driven and should not be annualized.
- Project execution, tariff, supply chain, and equipment bottleneck risk remain material.
- Segment-level FCF and product-level profitability are not disclosed.
- If power infrastructure multiples compress, a good operating result may still produce poor stock returns.

## What Would Change The Decision

- Upgrade toward WATCHLIST / selective ADD if price falls enough to lift FY2026 guided FCF yield materially.
- Upgrade if official results show recurring FCF above USD 7.5B without working-capital reversal.
- Keep WAIT if price remains near current levels and FCF stays within current guidance.
- Downgrade toward TRIM for oversized existing positions if price rises further without a matching FCF guide raise.
- Re-run DCF after Q2 2026 results, with special attention to Wind loss trajectory and customer advances.
