# JNJ Decision Memo - 2026-05-19

## Action Read

**Action: WAIT / AVOID new capital at current price.**

JNJ is a high-quality, cash-generative health care compounder with a durable dividend profile and a better Q1 2026 growth print than its mature-company label might imply. The issue is valuation. At USD 227.54, the stock prices in a lot of stability and cash-flow growth while official sources still show STELARA headwinds, MedTech margin pressure, litigation/legal overhang, higher net debt, and product-cycle execution risk.

For new capital, the action is **WAIT**. For an existing normal-sized defensive position, **HOLD** can be reasonable because dividend coverage and business quality remain intact, but the current price does not offer a DCF-based margin of safety.

The valuation does not say JNJ is a weak business. It says the current price requires a strong combination of FCF growth, successful launches, legal risk containment, and MedTech margin recovery.

## Bull Case

- Q1 2026 sales grew 9.9% reported and 6.4% operational.
- Management raised FY2026 sales and adjusted EPS guidance.
- FY2026 FCF outlook of approximately USD 21B supports dividend durability.
- Innovative Medicine growth is broad enough to offset part of STELARA pressure, with assets such as DARZALEX, CARVYKTI, ERLEADA, RYBREVANT/LAZCLUZE, TREMFYA, SPRAVATO, ICOTYDE, CAPLYTA, and INLEXZO.
- MedTech has growth drivers in electrophysiology, Abiomed, Shockwave, cardiovascular, trauma, and vision.
- A 64-year dividend growth record and 3.1% dividend increase support the quality case.

## Bear Case

- At USD 227.54, JNJ trades at about 26.1x FY2026 FCF outlook, which is not cheap for mature health care.
- STELARA is an explicit headwind, and management cited an approximate 540 bps Q1 operational sales headwind.
- MedTech margin declined, with tariffs and separation costs among the pressures.
- Net debt is approximately USD 32.936B after M&A and capital allocation.
- Legal/talc matters remain a major risk factor and can distort GAAP earnings.
- Product-level revenue and brand-level profitability for newer launch assets are not fully disclosed.

## What Would Change The Decision

- Upgrade toward add if price falls toward a materially higher FCF yield while official results remain stable.
- Upgrade if FY2026 FCF outlook rises meaningfully above USD 21B and net debt declines.
- Upgrade if launch assets show evidence of durable revenue growth and margin contribution beyond STELARA erosion.
- Upgrade if MedTech tariffs and separation costs fade without hurting growth.
- Downgrade toward trim if FCF disappoints, debt rises, litigation costs escalate, or guidance upgrades are not supported by cash flow.
