## Risk read-through

BSVO ลงทุนใน broad and diverse U.S. small-cap value stocks. Official 2024
prospectus ระบุว่าโดยปกติลงทุนอย่างน้อย 80% ในหุ้น small-cap และใช้ value
measures เช่น price-to-book, price-to-earnings, price-to-sales และ price-to-
cash-flow. Official 2025 annual report ระบุ 613 holdings และ 17% portfolio
turnover ณ 2025-06-30. ความเสี่ยงหลักคือ small-cap liquidity, value-regime,
sector concentration, market และ ESG-related risk. Official daily NAV history
สำหรับ maximum drawdown และ recovery ที่ทำซ้ำได้ยังไม่ถูก verify จึงไม่บันทึก
ตัวเลข drawdown.

## Active management read-through

- management_mode: active-equity-long-only
- active_process: systematic-quantitative
- management_benchmark: Russell 2000 Value Total Return Index
- track_record: established-with-predecessor-history
- management_evidence: mixed-benchmark-relative

- Official 2025 fiscal-year comparison shows -4.15 pp for one year, +4.98 pp for five-year annualized return and +0.42 pp for ten-year annualized return versus Russell 2000 Value TR. This is mixed benchmark-relative evidence, not alpha.
- The predecessor history is explicitly carried into the ETF performance table. The current ETF did not commence operations before the 2023-03-13 reorganization, and the official page states that predecessor returns may differ because the ETF’s net expense ratio is lower.
- The official strategy is statistical and evidence-based, ranking small-cap value stocks with market-cap weighting and risk constraints. Bridgeway’s product page identifies the team as John Montgomery, Elena Khoziaeva, Christine Wang and Andrew L. Berkin.
