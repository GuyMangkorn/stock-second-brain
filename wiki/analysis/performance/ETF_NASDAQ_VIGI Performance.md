# VIGI Performance

## Bottom Line

ใน common window 2021-2025 VIGI ให้ cumulative NAV total return `30.47%` หรือ
CAGR `5.46%`. ปีดีที่สุดคือ 2025 ที่ `+16.89%`; ปีบวกที่น้อยที่สุดคือ 2024 ที่
`+2.62%`; ปีแย่ที่สุดคือ 2022 ที่ `-16.71%`. ใน pilot VIGI เป็นกองที่ไวต่อ
international, FX และ country/regional risk มากที่สุดจากผลตอบแทน common window.

## Classification

- **Structural:** international dividend growth across developed and emerging
  markets excluding the United States; benchmark is the Spliced S&P Global
  Ex-U.S. Dividend Growers Index in USD NTR.
- **Behavioral:** higher FX/country sensitivity, lower upside capture and higher
  common-window downside than the U.S. dividend-growth pair.
- **Instrument-level role:** international dividend-growth diversifier; not a
  one-for-one substitute for DGRO or VIG.

## Driver Notes

- **2022, confirmed event:** global inflation, the Ukraine war and global rate
  tightening created a difficult environment. **Probable composition driver:**
  non-U.S. currency, regional and financial exposure amplified the common-window
  loss to `-16.71%`.
- **2020, confirmed event:** COVID caused a `-31.01%` secondary drawdown. The
  recovery was faster than VIG's full-history recovery, but the initial shock was
  still material.
- **2025, probable driver:** international/value and currency support helped VIGI
  recover to `+16.89%`; holdings-level attribution is still required before
  treating this as a repeatable edge.
