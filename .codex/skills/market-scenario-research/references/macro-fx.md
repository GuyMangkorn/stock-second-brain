# Macro And FX Scenarios

Use this reference for country economies, central banks, inflation, rates,
fiscal policy, capital flows, and currencies.

## Causal Map

Start with the requested horizon, then connect only material channels:

1. Growth, wages, inflation, productivity, and demographics
2. Central-bank reaction function and expected policy path
3. Nominal and real yield differentials versus relevant countries
4. Fiscal stance, debt issuance, and term premium
5. Current account, trade, energy imports, hedging, and portfolio flows
6. Currency valuation, positioning, risk sentiment, and intervention
7. Transmission to equities, exporters, importers, banks, and households

Do not infer a currency direction from one channel. A currency can weaken despite
tighter policy when foreign yields, risk flows, or fiscal concerns dominate.

## Preferred Evidence

Prioritize central-bank statements and forecasts, finance ministries, national
statistics agencies, debt-management offices, official trade/current-account
data, and international institutions. Use market data for current yields,
forward expectations, FX levels, and positioning with timestamps.

## Scenario Structure

- `Base`: policy and growth follow the most supported current path.
- `Bull / Upside`: define whether upside refers to growth, the currency, or the
  relevant asset; state the policy and flow combination required.
- `Bear / Downside`: identify the adverse inflation, growth, fiscal, or external
  shock and its transmission.

Avoid unsourced point forecasts. Use ranges only when a source or shown model
supports them.

## Signposts

Select three to five:

- core inflation, wage growth, consumption, or business investment
- central-bank guidance and market-implied policy path
- real-yield and nominal-yield differentials
- fiscal packages, issuance calendar, and term premium
- current account, trade balance, energy prices, and tourism
- FX intervention, hedging ratios, positioning, and volatility

State which signpost would falsify the base case first and the date of the next
scheduled data or policy checkpoint when known.
