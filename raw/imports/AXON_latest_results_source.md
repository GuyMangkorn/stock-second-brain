## Transcript / Commentary

- Management framed Q1 as Axon's ninth consecutive quarter of 30%+ growth, supported by TASER 10, Axon Body 4, counter-drone, real-time operations, and AI.
- Official release states Software & Services revenue grew 35% to USD 355M, AI products grew over 700% YoY, and counter-drone product revenue grew over 300% YoY.
- Transcript Q&A highlights adoption of AI offerings, large-event counter-drone demand, enterprise physical security use cases, pricing/packaging opportunities, and international/federal momentum.
- Management also said some memory-cost pressure is contemplated in guidance and Q1 working-capital drag was consistent with seasonal expectations.
