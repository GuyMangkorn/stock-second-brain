## Transcript / Commentary

- Management said Q3 delivered 6% organic revenue growth, 50 bps ahead of Q3 guidance, and reiterated FY26 organic revenue growth and non-GAAP EPS guidance.
- Cardiovascular growth was the largest driver, led by Cardiac Ablation Solutions and pulsed field ablation.
- Diabetes revenue grew double digits as reported; Medtronic had previously announced the intent to separate Diabetes into a standalone public company in the FY2025 Q4 release.
- Medtronic said it secured U.S. FDA clearance for Hugo robotic-assisted surgery, and highlighted M&A transactions including CathWorks and Anteris.
- The FY26 guide includes a potential tariff impact of approximately USD 185M; tariff policy remained a source of uncertainty in the Q3 10-Q.
