## Important Integrity Notes

- Analyst targets and screeners are lower-priority market context, not durable
  company facts.
- This pass did not normalize current price, market cap, net debt, FCF, or
  valuation for the full list.
- Do not treat a ticker's inclusion as a buy recommendation. It means the name
  deserves deeper research because the business setup and/or external screen
  suggests possible medium-to-high upside.
- For any ticker chosen for actual capital, next step should be a
  decision-grade workflow with fresh quote, latest filing, latest earnings
  release/transcript, normalized FCF, balance sheet, share count, and DCF.
