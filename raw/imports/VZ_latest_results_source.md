## Transcript / Commentary

- CEO Dan Schulman said Q1 2026 showed Verizon's turnaround gaining momentum and framed priorities around healthier growth, better customer economics, and stronger cash generation.
- Management said Q1 Mobility and Broadband service revenue included an 80 bps pressure to wireless service revenue growth from the January network outage.
- Management expects Q1 Mobility and Broadband service revenue to be the low point of 2026 and remains confident in 2% to 3% full-year growth.
- Verizon said it is shifting toward durable recurring service revenue and away from low-margin promotional activity.
- Management highlighted lower churn, lower acquisition/retention costs, customer-experience workstreams, AI/automation, digital sales/service channels, and an OpEx savings target of USD 5B in 2026.
- Frontier integration is on track, with a target of more than USD 1B run-rate operating cost synergies by 2028.
- Capital allocation priorities remain: network/platform investment, dividend, leverage reduction, and shareholder returns.
