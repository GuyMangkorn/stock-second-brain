## Transcript / Commentary

- Management framed Q1 2026 strength around focused structural heart execution, with TAVR, TMTT, and Surgical all growing.
- TAVR commentary emphasized SAPIEN platform demand, clinical evidence, proactive disease management, and faster OUS TAVR growth than U.S. TAVR, while management did not disclose exact U.S. TAVR growth.
- TMTT commentary emphasized the combined repair and replacement portfolio and early SAPIEN M3 rollout, but management described the launch as still early.
- CFO commentary in the transcript indicated R&D guidance around 17% of sales and tax-rate guidance of 16% to 19%, both non-GAAP / adjusted framing.
- Transcript is LSEG edited transcript hosted by IR; use it as management commentary and cross-check with SEC filings/release for financial numbers.
