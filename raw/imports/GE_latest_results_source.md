## Transcript / Commentary

- Management framed Q1 2026 as strong demand plus supply-chain execution: orders grew sharply, revenue was up double digits, and commercial services backlog was highlighted as a support for the full-year guide.
- GE maintained full-year 2026 guidance but said results were trending toward the high end, while still assuming elevated crude through 3Q, fuel availability pressure, lower global GDP assumptions, and flat to low-single-digit GE Aerospace/CFM departures growth.
- Management said there was no evidence of aftermarket customer pre-buying in Q1 and described spare-part/shop-visit demand as broad rather than one-off pull-forward.
- Supply chain remains a constraint: management discussed supplier input improvement but also acknowledged delinquency and on-time delivery as ongoing operational priorities.
- GE9X commentary indicated no change to schedule or losses in the Q1 call context, but program-level profitability is not separately disclosed.
