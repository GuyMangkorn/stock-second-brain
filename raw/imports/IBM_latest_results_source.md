## Transcript / Commentary

- CEO commentary: IBM framed the quarter as a Software-led Hybrid Cloud and AI platform story, with AI, hybrid cloud, governance, automation, Red Hat, and IBM Z as core demand drivers.
- AI positioning: management emphasized enterprise control of proprietary data, hybrid deployment across public/private/sovereign cloud and on-prem, model orchestration, governance, and security.
- Red Hat / Software: Software constant-currency growth was 8%; Red Hat grew 10% constant currency and OpenShift was described as a USD 2B ARR business.
- Consulting: Consulting revenue grew 1% constant currency; signings returned to growth at 6%; generative AI represented about 30% of Consulting backlog.
- Infrastructure: IBM Z grew 48% constant currency in Q1 2026 and management described z17 as continuing to outperform prior programs.
- Productivity: management cited USD 4.5B of productivity savings since 2023 and expected an additional USD 1B in 2026.
