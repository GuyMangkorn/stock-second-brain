## Transcript / Commentary

- Management said Q2 FY2026 net revenue grew 17% and EPS grew 20%, with the highest net revenue growth since 2022.
- CEO Ryan McInerney framed Visa as a payments "hyperscaler" and highlighted consumer payments, commercial and money movement, agentic commerce, stablecoins, blockchain settlement, and value-added services.
- Visa Direct reached 3.7B transactions in Q2, up 23% YoY.
- Value-added services revenue grew 27% constant dollar to USD 3.3B; management said VAS represents about 30% of net revenue.
- CFO Christopher Suh said Q2 outperformance came from higher-than-expected volatility, stronger value-added services revenue, and lower-than-expected incentives.
- Management noted the Middle East conflict as a near-term uncertainty for CEMEA cross-border travel spend.
- Visa repurchased USD 7.9B of stock in Q2 FY2026, distributed USD 1.3B in dividends, and had approximately USD 33B total buyback capacity after the April authorization.
- The May 2026 Class B exchange offer is material for share structure, but the latest SEC 10-Q diluted share count and market-data provider market cap remain the most usable inputs for valuation.
