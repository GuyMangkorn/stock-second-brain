## Transcript / Commentary

Official prepared remarks described Q1 2026 as a stronger execution quarter after actions taken in late 2025. Management emphasized pricing discipline, medical cost management, member mix, AI-enabled modernization, cybersecurity/customer-experience investment, and balance sheet discipline.

Source-backed commentary points:

- All major operating segments exceeded internal plan in Q1 2026, with management calling out Medicare and Optum Health strength.
- Reported medical care ratio improved to 83.9% from 84.8% in Q1 2025, helped by pricing, cost management, and favorable reserve development.
- Management cautioned that some Q1 benefits, including respiratory seasonality and Part D seasonality dynamics, should moderate through the year.
- The company expects full-year 2026 adjusted diluted EPS greater than USD 18.25 and GAAP diluted EPS greater than USD 17.35.
- Management expects about two-thirds of 2026 earnings in the first half, with UnitedHealthcare and Optum Health especially front-half weighted.
- The company expects to repurchase at least USD 2.0B of common stock by the end of Q2 2026.
- The official source set included prepared remarks, but not a company-hosted full Q&A transcript.
