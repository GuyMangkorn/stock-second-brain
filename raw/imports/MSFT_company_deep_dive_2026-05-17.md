## Research Takeaways

- Business model: Microsoft monetizes enterprise and consumer software subscriptions, cloud consumption, on-premises licenses, LinkedIn subscriptions/ads, Dynamics applications, gaming content/subscriptions, Windows OEM licensing, devices, and search advertising.
- Revenue mix: FY2025 was led by Productivity and Business Processes at 42.88% of revenue and Intelligent Cloud at 37.72%; FY26 Q3 shifted further toward cloud, with Productivity and Business Processes and Intelligent Cloud together at 84.08% of quarterly revenue.
- Moat indicators: enterprise installed base, cloud scale, identity/security/compliance integration, developer ecosystem, Work IQ/data context inside Microsoft 365, GitHub usage, Windows distribution, and capital capacity for datacenter buildout.
- Key debate: reported AI/cloud demand is strong, but the investment case depends on whether high capex, finance leases, depreciation, and GPU/CPU costs convert into durable revenue, margins, and free cash flow.
- Valuation: no current stock price, market capitalization, valuation multiples, or analyst targets were ingested in this source note; those require a fresh market-data check before valuation use.
