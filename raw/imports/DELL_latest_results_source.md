## Transcript / Commentary

- Management framed FY2026 as a record year with FY2026 revenue of USD 113.5B, non-GAAP EPS of USD 10.30, cash flow from operations above USD 11B, and capital returns of USD 7.5B including roughly 54M shares repurchased.
- AI server demand is the core forward driver: management said FY2026 AI orders were USD 64.1B, AI shipments were about USD 25B, and ending AI backlog was USD 43B.
- The transcript states management expects FY2027 AI revenue of roughly USD 50B and notes supply tightness, higher input costs, shorter quote validity, price increases, and margin protection actions.
- For cash flow, management stated the company does not guide to cash, while expecting another strong year; therefore FY2027 FCF is not treated as disclosed guidance.
