## Transcript / Commentary

- Satya Nadella said Microsoft Cloud exceeded $54 billion in revenue, up 29% year over year, and that the AI business surpassed a $37 billion annual revenue run rate, up 123% year over year.
- Management described two priorities: cloud and AI infrastructure for agentic computing, and high-value agentic systems across productivity, coding, security, and other domains.
- Amy Hood said Q3 revenue was $82.9 billion, up 18% and 15% in constant currency; operating income increased 20% and 16% in constant currency; adjusted EPS increased 21% and 18% in constant currency when excluding the OpenAI investment impact.
- Amy Hood said Q3 capital expenditures were $31.9 billion, with roughly two thirds for short-lived assets, primarily GPUs and CPUs; cash paid for property and equipment was $30.9 billion.
- Microsoft guided Q4 FY26 total revenue to $86.7 billion to $87.8 billion and expected calendar 2026 capital expenditures of roughly $190 billion, including approximately $25 billion from higher component pricing.
