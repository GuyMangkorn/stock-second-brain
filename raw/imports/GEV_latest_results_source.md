## Transcript / Commentary

- Management said Q1 results gave the company confidence to raise 2026 adjusted EBITDA and FCF guidance, with Power and Electrification driving the positive revision.
- Power demand is being helped by gas turbine demand, service backlog, and data-center / electrification load growth, but Gas Power revenue timing can be lumpy.
- Electrification demand remains strong, including Grid Solutions and Prolec GE. Management noted elevated backlog and strong orders.
- Wind remains the main turnaround / execution risk. Management highlighted onshore restructuring, offshore project losses, and an expectation for Wind to improve but remain negative in 2026.
- The Q1 FCF result benefited from orders, customer down payments, and slot reservation agreements. This is source-backed but not safe to annualize mechanically.
