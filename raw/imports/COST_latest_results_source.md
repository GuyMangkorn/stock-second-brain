## Transcript / Commentary

Management commentary from the Q2 FY2026 call was used for thesis context, not as a replacement for filing facts:

- Tariff impact remained fluid; management said buyers were trying to reduce price impact through sourcing changes, global buying consolidation, Kirkland Signature control, and more domestic sourcing.
- Management expected 28 net new openings in FY2026 and targeted 30+ new openings per year in coming years.
- CFO commentary put full-year FY2026 capex at approximately USD 6.5B.
- Management said digital roadmap work was improving warehouse checkout, pharmacy pay-ahead, employee pre-scan, automated pay-station pilots, personalization, and digitally-enabled sales.
- Category commentary highlighted fresh foods, non-foods, food and sundries, pharmacy, food court, optical, and digitally-enabled sales strength.
