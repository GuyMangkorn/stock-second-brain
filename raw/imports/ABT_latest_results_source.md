## Transcript / Commentary

Abbott described Q1 2026 results as aligned with expectations and highlighted the March 23, 2026 acquisition of Exact Sciences as creating a new Cancer Diagnostics business. Management framed Medical Devices and Established Pharmaceuticals as key growth contributors, while Nutrition remained pressured by lower volumes and prior strategic pricing actions.

Official release commentary says:

- Medical Devices sales increased 13.2% reported and 8.5% comparable.
- Established Pharmaceuticals sales increased 13.2% reported and 9.0% comparable.
- Diagnostics sales increased 6.1% reported and 1.8% comparable.
- Nutrition sales decreased 6.0% reported and 7.7% comparable.
- Cancer Diagnostics reported USD 96M of sales from the acquisition date.

Full Q&A transcript was not normalized in this P1 note because an official company-hosted transcript was not found in the extracted source set. The official webcast reference exists through Abbott Investor Relations, but durable facts here use filings and the official release.
