## Transcript / Commentary

- Official release commentary: Q3 FY2026 sales growth was broad, with total organic sales up 3% and all five operating segments positive on reported sales.
- Margin commentary: reported gross margin declined 150 bps; core gross margin declined 100 bps. The company cited unfavorable mix, reinvestments, tariffs, commodity cost, and rounding/other items, partly offset by productivity savings and pricing.
- Guidance commentary: P&G maintained FY2026 sales and EPS ranges but now expects EPS results toward the lower end because of commodity costs, tariffs, interest/tax headwinds, FX tailwind, and increased innovation / demand-creation investments.
- Transcript context from secondary source: management discussed volume recovery, North America / Europe / Greater China organic growth, market-share stabilization, productivity offsets, and cash returns. Because the full official transcript was not verified, this is lower-priority context and not treated as a primary financial fact.
