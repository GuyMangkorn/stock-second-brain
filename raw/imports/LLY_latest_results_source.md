### Q1 2026 product and operating facts

- Total revenue grew 56%; reported volume effect was +65%, price effect was -13%, and FX effect was +4%.
- U.S. revenue was USD 12,119m (+43%) and outside-U.S. revenue was USD 7,680m (+81%).
- Mounjaro revenue was USD 8,662m (+125%); Zepbound revenue was USD 4,160m (+80%); Verzenio was USD 1,302m (+12%); Jardiance was USD 1,114m (+10%); Trulicity was USD 919m (-16%); Taltz was USD 733m (-4%).
- Gross margin was 81.9% reported / 82.6% non-GAAP; R&D was USD 3,510m (+28%); marketing, selling and administrative expense was USD 2,934m (+19%); reported operating income was USD 8,915m.
- Lilly's earnings materials report U.S. incretin analog market share of 60.1% and international share of 53.2% using IQVIA data as of March/April 2026; these are company-presented market data, not SEC financial statement facts.
- Foundayo (orforglipron) received U.S. FDA approval for obesity; the company also highlighted positive Phase 3 data for orforglipron and retatrutide, and pipeline progress for Jaypirca and Ebglyss.
