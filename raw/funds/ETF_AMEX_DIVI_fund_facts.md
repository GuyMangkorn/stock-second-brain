## Index Methodology

The underlying index begins with Morningstar's Developed Markets ex-North
America Target Market Exposure Index, targeting large- and mid-cap stocks in the
top 85% of the float-adjusted investable universe. An optimizer seeks higher
trailing-twelve-month dividend yield while limiting expected tracking error to
the parent index.

At quarterly reconstitution, the prospectus specifies stock-weight caps, sector
weights within 5% of the parent, country weights within 5% and no more than
three times the parent weight, and a 10% one-way turnover cap unless a feasible
optimal solution requires relaxation. The index is not a pure high-yield screen;
some constituents may not currently pay dividends because of the core and
tracking-error constraints. The fund may use futures, total-return swaps, and
foreign-currency forwards for implementation and tracking.

## Risks & Gaps

- Financials are the largest sector at `29.13%`, so the fund is not a neutral
  developed-markets core despite holding 417 names.
- Japan is the largest country at `23.93%`; unhedged foreign-currency exposure,
  country risk, withholding taxes, and local-market liquidity matter.
- The index uses an optimizer and may include non-dividend payers; a dividend
  tilt does not guarantee stable or rising distributions.
- Sampling and permitted futures/swaps/FX forwards can create tracking
  differences; the prospectus explicitly discloses non-correlation and index
  risks.
